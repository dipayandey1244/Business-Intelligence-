# Business case: client intelligence for CIB

## Illustrative user problem
Relationship managers and analytics teams may need a consistent way to triage a large portfolio, form hypotheses about product needs, and see what evidence is missing before a relationship review.

## Proposed workflow
1. Combine approved client and relationship attributes in a governed data mart.
2. Produce transparent descriptive segments and propensity estimates.
3. Prioritise records for human review using documented criteria.
4. Present reasons, uncertainty, and evidence gaps in an interactive interface.
5. Capture review outcomes and monitor usefulness, fairness, drift, and unintended effects.

## Value hypotheses to test
- Less time spent assembling portfolio review lists.
- More consistent prioritisation and clearer evidence trails.
- Better separation of predictive scores from causal claims.
- Easier challenge and review through documented assumptions.

These are hypotheses, not measured outcomes. This demo does not establish business impact or reproduce any bank's internal workflow.

## Success metrics for a real pilot
Measure time-to-review, proportion of prompts judged relevant by coverage, calibration and ranking performance, incremental outcomes using an appropriate experimental design, override rates, subgroup performance, data freshness, and documented client-impact safeguards. Establish baselines and acceptance thresholds before deployment.
